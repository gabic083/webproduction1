About the Author

Writing Outside the Lines. Independent voices and unconventional stories.

Aina Hunter is an American writer whose work has appeared across a range of independent publications, alternative weeklies, and magazines. Her writing credits include The Village Voice, Mother Jones, Essence, Glamour, and Black Enterprise, among others. With a background that crosses journalism, cultural writing, and independent publishing, Hunter has built a career outside the more traditional literary establishment.

Hunter's work has appeared in publications that have historically made space for voices and perspectives that might not always find a home in mainstream media. Her writing career reflects an interest in the less conventional corners of culture, and in the possibilities of independent journalism and storytelling. She has also received recognition from organizations including Columbia University, UC Berkeley, and the National Association of Black Journalists.

Alongside her journalism, Hunter is the author of Charlotte and the Chickenman, published through the independent press Whisk(e)y Tit. Her move into book writing adds another dimension to a career that has never been confined to one particular format. From alternative newspapers to independent books, Hunter represents a kind of literary practice that values experimentation, independence, and the freedom to tell stories outside the expected.
