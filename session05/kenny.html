Devin Kenny

Ongoing, Individual Adaptability or How to Quiet Quit

2022

Ongoing, Individual Adaptability or How to Quiet Quit is a project that uses digital images, collages, videos, and AI. Kenny trained a generative AI system using images connected to exhibitions at the Whitney Museum and then used the results to create new collages. The project looks at artificial intelligence, creativity, and work, while also questioning what role humans have when technology can create images and ideas.
