Addie Wagenknecht

Believe Me

2017

Believe Me explores trust and the way technology can affect what people believe. The project uses digital imagery and technology to make the viewer question what they are seeing and whether an image or piece of information can really be trusted. It connects to the way information is presented online and how technology can make something seem believable even when we do not know if it is true.
