Independent Literary Publications

The internet has created space for independent literary magazines, experimental fiction, underground writing, and alternative literary communities. These publications challenge traditional ideas about where literature belongs and who gets to publish it.

Hobart is an independent literary publication featuring fiction, essays, and poetry. Its literary world embraces emerging voices, unconventional storytelling, and writing that exists outside the mainstream publishing industry.
